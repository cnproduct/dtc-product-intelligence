### The 3 Golden Rules of DTC Product Selection:
1. **The 3-Second Scroll-Stopping Rule**: If a consumer cannot grasp the transformation, problem, or visual shock within 3 seconds of a silent TikTok/Reels video, the ad will suffer fatal CPA inflation.
2. **The 4x-6x Margin Floor**: Product selling price must be at least 4.0x – 6.0x the landed manufacturing cost (FOB + shipping). Anything below 3.5x will lose money after ad acquisition costs (CAC) and merchant fees.
3. **The Logistics Weight Boundary**: Keep unit shipping weight under 500g whenever possible. This unlocks ultra-fast, cheap air packet lines (6–9 days to US/EU at under $4.00 per package).

- **Jinjiang / Quanzhou (泉州晋江鞋服与箱包带)**: World leader in seamless shapewear, windbreakers, running gear, and tactical anti-theft bags.
