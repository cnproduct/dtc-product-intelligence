### 2. Multi-Platform Data Convergence Checklist
Check off verified platform signals (Must satisfy at least 3 out of 5):
- [ ] **Amazon**: In Movers & Shakers (>500% rank jump) or Hot New Releases Top 20.
- [ ] **Temu**: High sales velocity (>10,000 orders) with 4.7+ star review baseline.
- [ ] **SHEIN / AliExpress**: Trending search volume or SuperDeals top reorder list.
- [ ] **DTC Ad Spy (PiPiADS / Minea / AdSpy)**: Active ads running > 7 days with >1,000,000 impressions.
- [ ] **Google Trends**: 7d/30d search trajectory slope > +150% or Breakout status.

### 5. Go / No-Go Decision Gate
- [ ] **GO (Scale)**: DVI >= 90. Build high-converting Shopify single-product landing page, deploy 10 UGC video variations, order initial batch.
- [ ] **TEST (Agile Dropship)**: DVI 80–89. Test 3 ad angles with small $50/day ad spend, fulfill via air direct packet.
- [ ] **NO-GO (Reject)**: Margin < 3.2x, fragile glass, trademark infringement, or Temu retail price is lower than our factory FOB.
